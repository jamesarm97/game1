The Cowboy and Cat

A campfire story

Out where the hills are orange and the sky goes on forever, there lived a cowboy named Dusty.

Dusty had a tall hat, a slow horse named Pickle, and nobody to talk to.

Every night Dusty sat by his fire and talked to the stars. The stars never talked back.

But one night, two green eyes blinked in the dark.

In the morning, Dusty reached for his hat. There was a cat in it.

“That’s my hat,” said Dusty. The cat yawned. It was her hat now.

“Cowboys don’t have cats,” said Dusty, and he rode away.

Clip, clop, went Pickle. Pit, pat, went the cat, right behind.

Then Pickle stopped. Tk-tk-tk! A rattlesnake lay across the trail, shaking its tail.

Pickle would not take one more step. Dusty’s knees went wobbly.

But the cat was not wobbly. She puffed up as big as a tumbleweed and said, HISSSS!

The snake took one look and wiggled away as fast as it could go.

“Well, I’ll be,” said Dusty. “I reckon a cowboy can have a cat.”

He named her Biscuit, because she was warm and golden and just what he needed.

From then on, they rode together. Dusty talked, and Biscuit listened.

Sometimes she even talked back. “Mrrow,” she said, which means, “Keep going, partner.”

That night, Dusty did not talk to the stars. He had a friend to say goodnight to.

“Goodnight, Biscuit.” And the cowboy and the cat fell fast asleep.

The End
